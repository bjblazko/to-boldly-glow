A: Two Rails

Idea: a desktop-app layout. The left rail answers "what am I looking at?" (objects, lessons). The right rail answers "what is shown?" (layers). Time is a strip along the bottom, camera is a small cluster bottom-right. Graphics (renderer settings) leave the main UI for a settings dialog behind ⚙. Both rails can be collapsed to icons; on a phone they become bottom sheets.
