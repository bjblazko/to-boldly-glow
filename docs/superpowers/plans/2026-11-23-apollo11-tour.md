# The "Apollo 11" tour, first version – implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A narrated-by-captions, ~30-minute tour of Apollo 11 from Earth orbit to splashdown at true
scale on the reconstructed path, with a film-like camera, inside views through the windows and the
controls worked at the decisive moments.

**Architecture:** A pure mission clock maps tour seconds to mission time (GET) per station (sped up,
real-time windows, fades between stations). A scene player stages Earth, Moon and Sun at true scale
(1 unit = 1,000 km) around a floating origin at the craft in view, and draws the craft (and later
the cockpits and Tranquility's terrain) in a metre pass: camera-relative, its own near/far, its
depth squeezed into [0, 0.001] so it lies in front of every body in the one depth buffer. A shot
system and director pick varied shots per station; the orbit camera is steered to them.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, WebGPU, gl-matrix, Vitest, Playwright; existing `ModelRenderer`,
`ScenePlayer`, mission tour UI, narration captions.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-apollo11-tour-design.md`

### Task 1: The mission clock

**Files:**
- Create: `packages/app/src/missions/apollo11/apollo11Clock.ts`
- Test: `packages/app/test/apollo11Clock.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `interface TourStation { id: string; from: number; to: number; seconds: number; realTime?: { from: number; to: number }[] }` (GET seconds; `seconds`: its length in the tour)
  - `interface ClockMoment { stationIndex: number; get: number; fade: number; paused: boolean; ended: boolean }`
  - `class Apollo11Clock { constructor(stations: TourStation[], listener: { arrived(i: number): void; ended(): void }, animate: boolean); start(): void; jumpTo(i: number): void; setPaused(p: boolean): void; get isPaused(): boolean; update(dt: number): ClockMoment; get moment(): ClockMoment }`
- Pace: inside a station GET runs at 1× in its real-time windows; the rest of the station's time is spread over the remaining tour seconds with a smooth speed curve (no jumps in GET, speed changes eased over ≥1 s). Between stations a fade of 0.7 s out and in (fade = 1 at the cut), GET cuts to the next station's `from`.

- [ ] Step 1: tests - `GET starts at the station's from and ends at its to`; `runs at real time inside a real-time window` (dGET/dt = 1 ± 0.01 there); `GET never decreases within a station`; `jumpTo(7) starts at stations[7].from`; `pause holds GET; a pause requested during a fade waits for its end`; `without animation each station still lasts its seconds and the tour ends`; `the station's tour length is its seconds ± 0.1 s`.
- [ ] Step 2: run, see them fail. `npx vitest run test/apollo11Clock.test.ts` → FAIL (module missing).
- [ ] Step 3: implement. Per station precompute a piecewise map tour-seconds → GET: real-time windows take their own length; the remaining GET is spread over the remaining seconds, the speed eased (smoothstep) in and out of each window.
- [ ] Step 4: run → PASS; lint.
- [ ] Step 5: commit.

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:

Attitudes: TLI and LOI/TEI along/against the velocity as burned (SPS forward: retrograde for LOI, prograde for TEI), PTC on the coasts (a slow roll, 0.3°/s, APPROX), the LM's descent from the descent profile's pitch (APPROX), the CM heat shield first at entry.
