To Boldly Glow {version}
========================

A planetarium of the Solar System in your browser. This copy runs from your disk, without a web
server and without an internet connection.

How to start: unzip, then open index.html (double-click it, or drag it into a browser window).

It needs a browser with WebGPU. It runs fastest in a Chrome-based browser: Chrome, Edge, Chromium
or Helium. Safari 26 or newer works, but more slowly. Firefox works only on Windows and on Macs with
Apple silicon - not on Linux or Android. On an iPhone or iPad, any browser on iOS/iPadOS 26 or
newer works.

Your settings and where you left each lesson stay in this browser only.
