To Boldly Glow {version}
========================

A planetarium of the Solar System in your browser. This copy runs from your disk, without a web
server and without an internet connection.

How to start: unzip, then open index.html (double-click it, or drag it into a browser window).

It needs a browser with WebGPU, for example a current Chrome or Edge, Safari 26 or newer, or a
current Firefox. If the page says WebGPU is missing, try another browser.

Your settings (language, voice, lessons you finished) stay in this browser only.

Licenses: the code is MIT-licensed; third-party images, data and fonts keep their own licenses -
see the About dialog (the (i) button at the top right) and the licenses folder.
More: https://huepattl.de/products/to-boldly-glow
